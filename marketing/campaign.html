The goal

A marketplace is only useful when both sides are there. So we launch in one city and make it feel full: places first, then people. Bengaluru has the most flatsharing, the most new-joiners every month, and our seed listings. Pune follows in January once these numbers hold.

Who we’re talking to

Gen Z is the core, but the copy stays plain and friendly so it works for everyone who rents.

Core · 18–24

Students & interns

Need a hostel or PG near college or their first office, on a tight budget. Live on Instagram and WhatsApp. Fear: ending up with a nightmare roommate.

Core · 22–30

First-jobbers & movers

Just relocated to Bengaluru for work. Want a decent flatshare in Koramangala, HSR or Whitefield fast. Hate brokers and spam calls. On Instagram, LinkedIn, Reddit.

Supply

Owners, PGs & hosts

PG operators, people with a spare room, small co-living brands, BnB hosts. Want reliable tenants without paying brokerage. Reach them on WhatsApp, Facebook groups and in person.

Secondary

Couples & families

Want an entire flat, or a short BnB stay while house-hunting in a new city. Reach through Google Search and Facebook.

What we say

One line

Swipe on rooms and roommates. Kinnesto matches you by budget, timing and how you actually live, with no brokers.

1 · Vibe match

Night owl or 6am runner? Veg or biryani Sundays? Every card shows a match % and the reasons. Proof: the score on screen.

2 · No brokers

Chat directly with owners and flatmates. Free for everyone. Proof: no fees anywhere in the app.

3 · Every kind of home

Flats, PGs, hostels, co-living and BnB stays in one place. Proof: the five tabs on the Places screen.

4 · Safer by design

Area shown, never the exact address. Block & report in two taps. Clear safety tips. Never claim “verified” until verification exists.

Voice: warm, quick, a little cheeky, never cringe. Hinglish is welcome in Reels and captions (“Flat mila? Flatmate bhi milega.”), plain English on ads and the website.

Four phases

11–15 Nov · Prep

Seed the supply

  • Personally onboard 50 Bengaluru PGs, hosts and spare rooms (WhatsApp script below, plus walking visits in Koramangala, HSR, BTM and Whitefield).
  • Set up Instagram @kinnesto, a Facebook page, LinkedIn page and Google Business Profile.
  • Recruit 10 college ambassadors (Christ, RV, PES, BMS, NMIMS Bengaluru).
  • Add analytics and UTM tracking.
16–22 Nov · Launch week

Make noise

  • Founder launch post on LinkedIn and Instagram.
  • Daily Reels (scripts below).
  • Posts in flatshare groups and r/bangalore, following each group’s rules.
  • Switch on Google Search and Meta ads at a small daily budget.
23 Nov – 6 Dec · Grow

Double down on what works

  • Move budget to the best ad sets and keywords.
  • 5 micro-influencer collabs (5k–50k followers: Bengaluru living, student life, relocation).
  • Posters with QR codes near tech parks and colleges.
  • “Bring your flatmate” referral push.
7–15 Dec · Prove

Stories and retention

  • Share real match stories (with permission).
  • Re-engage unfinished profiles.
  • Press pitch to YourStory, Inc42 and Bangalore Mirror.
  • Write up results and decide on the Pune launch.

Reel scripts

Reel 1 · Flatmate red flags (15–20 s)
On-screen: "POV: you picked your flatmate from a WhatsApp group"
Shots: dishes piling up (Day 3) · 2am karaoke (Day 7) · "bro my cousin is staying for a month" (Day 12)
Cut to phone: swiping on Kinnesto, card shows "92% vibe · Both early birds · Neat freaks"
On-screen: "Or… match by vibe first."
End card: kinnesto.com · Swipe on rooms and roommates
Caption: Tell us your worst flatmate story 👇 (we built Kinnesto because of ours) #bangalore #flatmates #pglife
Reel 2 · “I built a dating app for flatmates” (20–30 s, founder to camera)
Hook (0–2 s): "I made a dating app… but for finding flatmates."
2–10 s: screen recording: swipe right on a person, "It's a match: Roomies? 🏡"
10–20 s: show the vibe score reasons: "Both veg, both night owls, similar budget"
20–25 s: Places tab: PGs, flats, hostels, BnBs. "And no brokers. Free."
CTA: "It's live in Bengaluru. Link in bio."
Caption: Flat mila? Flatmate bhi milega. 😌 Live now in Bengaluru, kinnesto.com
Reel 3 · Broker vs Kinnesto (split screen, 15 s)
Left "Broker": 14 missed calls · "1 month brokerage" · "Sir flat already gone"
Right "Kinnesto": swipe → host accepts → chat open
Text: "No brokers. No spam calls. Just vibes."
Caption: Which side are you on? 😅 Free on kinnesto.com

How we’ll know it’s working

Track

  • Sign-ups and profile completion
  • Listings posted (by type and area)
  • Swipes per active user
  • Matches + accepted requests
  • Cost per completed profile, by channel

Before launch: the site doesn’t have analytics yet. Add Google Analytics 4 or PostHog plus the Meta Pixel and Google Ads tag, so the numbers above can actually be measured.

Rules we don’t break

Truth in ads

No fake reviews, numbers or testimonials. Don’t call users “verified” until verification exists. Use real stories only with permission.

Influencers

Every paid or gifted post carries #ad or #collab, as ASCI’s influencer guidelines require.

Communities

Read each group’s and subreddit’s rules first, disclose that you’re the founder, and never spam.

Privacy

Message only people who opted in. Follow the DPDP Act and the privacy policy at kinnesto.com/privacy.
