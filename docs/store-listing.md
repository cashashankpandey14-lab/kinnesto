**Full description:**

Find your place in Mumbai. And the people to share it with.

Kinnesto brings flats, PGs, hostels, co-living and short stays together with flatmate matching, so you can find a room and the right people to live with in one app. No brokers, no spam calls.

SWIPE ON PLACES
Browse rooms, PGs, hostels, co-living and stays across Andheri, Powai, Bandra, Lower Parel, Thane and the rest of Mumbai. Swipe right on the ones you like. Every place shows how well it fits your budget, area and move-in date.

MATCH WITH FLATMATES
Tell us how you live: sleep schedule, food habits, budget, cleanliness, guests. Kinnesto shows your match score with every potential flatmate before you ever meet.

CHAT DIRECTLY
When you match, chat with owners and flatmates directly in the app and plan a visit. Zero brokerage.

LIST YOUR ROOM FREE
Owners, PGs and co-living spaces can list a place in two minutes. Requests arrive with a profile and a match score, so you choose who moves in.

SAFE BY DESIGN
Report and block anyone in one tap. Our team reviews every report. You can delete your account at any time from the app.

Kinnesto is live in Mumbai, with more cities coming soon.
